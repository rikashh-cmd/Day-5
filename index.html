Araliya Hotels & Resorts

Elegance, Unmatched Luxury

As a flagship conglomerate dedicated to transforming Sri Lanka’s travel and hospitality industry, Araliya Hotels & Resorts has meticulously designed and developed some of the finest hotels in the country. The name “Araliya,” symbolizing a flower with an enchanting fragrance and captivating appearance, serves as our inspiration. Drawing from this beautiful blossom, we at Araliya Hotels & Resorts ensure that our guests experience the height of luxurious living.

Our resorts are staffed by hospitable and highly talented individuals who are committed to providing unparalleled service. We pride ourselves on offering competitive, best-in-class hospitality at our various resort establishments, each set in unique and iconic locations around the island. At Araliya Hotels & Resorts, we strive to create unforgettable experiences, allowing our patrons to immerse themselves in the beauty and culture of Sri Lanka while enjoying world-class amenities and exceptional service.
